# Email design

The email is a reading surface for private market monitoring. Use a restrained editorial report with a warm neutral background, white reading surface, dark green-black text and muted teal emphasis. It needs no hero metric, illustrations, remote fonts, tracking assets or scripts.

Use one column at all widths, capped at 680px. Inline base styles and presentation tables keep the email readable when advanced CSS is unavailable. Body text is 16px; secondary facts are 14px with strong contrast. The heading scale is 26/22/18px. Use system fonts with Traditional Chinese fallbacks.

Lead with both home summaries, then each home's dated sales and current asking evidence. Separate rows with fine rules; avoid nested cards, wide data tables and decorative badges. A zero-data section explains the evidence gap. Rank scores, mixed medians and purchase-price anchors do not lead the report. Wrap long addresses and preserve plain-text content parity.

Candidates are inspected at desktop and narrow phone widths. Browser render evidence does not establish Gmail/Outlook compatibility or inbox delivery. One final demonstration email is sent only after both product and design review accept the final candidate.
